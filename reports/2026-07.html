What the numbers say

CHF 184M of the month's capital, 59%, is public market money rather than venture: MoonLake Immunotherapeutics (Follow-on, USD 230M). Venture capital proper was CHF 112.4M across 9 rounds.

The two largest deals are 77% of the month's capital, so the total describes a handful of companies rather than the market.

The median round is CHF 7.1M against a mean of CHF 25.9M. The gap is the month in one line: a long tail of small rounds under a few large ones.

6 of 16 rounds were pre-seed or seed, and together they drew 11% of the capital. The formation end is busy and cheap.

10 of 16 rounds came out of an institution (ETH Zurich 5, EPFL 2, CSEM 2, Imperial College London 1), which is the structural feature of this market rather than a feature of the month.

33 distinct investors were named across 12 rounds. Only 3 appear more than once: Zürcher Kantonalbank, Swisscom Ventures, Venture Kick.
