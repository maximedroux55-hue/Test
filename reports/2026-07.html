What the numbers say

CHF 184M of the month's capital, 50%, is public market money rather than venture: MoonLake Immunotherapeutics (Follow-on, USD 230M). Venture capital proper was CHF 137.1M across 15 rounds.

The two largest deals are 65% of the month's capital, so the total describes a handful of companies rather than the market.

The median round is CHF 4.2M against a mean of CHF 17.5M. The gap is the month in one line: a long tail of small rounds under a few large ones.

9 of 25 rounds were pre-seed or seed, and together they drew 10% of the capital. The formation end is busy and cheap.

9 of 25 rounds came out of an institution (ETH Zurich 5, EPFL 2, CSEM 2), which is the structural feature of this market rather than a feature of the month.

33 distinct investors were named across 21 rounds. Only 4 appear more than once: Venture Kick, Swisscom Ventures, Zürcher Kantonalbank, OCCIDENT.

6 of 25 closed rounds have been checked against a primary source, covering CHF 297M of the CHF 366.6M, 81%. The rest is as reported. Every error found in this database so far was a figure or a label that a primary source contradicted, so that share is the weight this page can carry.
