Drop a Crunchbase funding-rounds export here. It is checked in your browser first, so you see exactly what would be added before anything is saved.

What this does

The tracker only knows what its sources carry, and no newsroom writes up an Innosuisse or Venture Kick award. An export fills that gap.

Buyouts, debt and equity crowdfunding are left out. Everything else becomes a round and joins the same merge as the rest, so a round already on record gains the export's facts rather than appearing twice.

The file is saved into the repository and re-read on every run, so it keeps working rather than being a one-off import.

Saved to deeptech-news/submissions/ on the site's repository. Nothing is uploaded until you press Save.
