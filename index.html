About

Patient capital for Swiss deep technology

I am a General Partner at Climb Ventures SA, a Geneva-based, FINMA-authorized venture capital firm focused on Swiss DeepTech scale-ups. Climb backs a focused set of companies, pairing patient capital with hands-on support to help world-class Swiss research become businesses built to scale globally.

Before venture, I co-founded Talent.com (formerly neuvoo) in Montreal and served as co-CEO, growing it over 13 years into a global recruitment platform. Earlier in my career I worked in finance in London.
