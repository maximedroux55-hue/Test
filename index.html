About

Patient capital for Swiss deep technology.

I am a General Partner at Climb Ventures SA, a Geneva-based, FINMA-authorized venture capital firm focused on Swiss DeepTech scale-ups. My work centers on capital-efficient companies turning Swiss research into global businesses.

Climb backs a focused set of Swiss DeepTech scale-ups, pairing patient capital with hands-on support to help category-defining companies scale globally from Switzerland.

Before venture, I co-founded Talent.com (formerly neuvoo) in Montreal and served as co-CEO, growing it over 13 years into a global recruitment platform. Earlier in my career I worked in finance in London.
