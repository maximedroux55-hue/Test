Scientists at @EPFL have identified a lipid mechanism that blocks anthrax toxin from entering cells. It is fundamental cell biology with an immediate line of sight to therapeutic and biodefence applications.

Why it matters:
🧬 Host-targeted approaches to toxins and pathogens open a different drug development pathway, one that is less vulnerable to resistance and increasingly attractive to pharma partners looking beyond conventional antibiotics.
🇨🇭 Switzerland combines elite basic research with the densest life sciences industrial cluster in Europe, so the distance between a published mechanism and a licensing conversation is unusually short here.
🛡️ What it enables: biodefence and infectious disease preparedness have moved up the agenda for governments, and mechanistic insight of this kind is the raw material for that pipeline.

The Swiss model works because discovery, translation and manufacturing sit within a two-hour train ride of each other. That proximity is an underrated form of capital efficiency.
