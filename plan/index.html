🇨🇭 Swiss open AI infrastructure enters its second act

@EPFL has unveiled Apertus 1.5, the next iteration of Switzerland's open large language model effort, built on public research infrastructure and released for the community to inspect, adapt and build on.

Why it matters:
🌍 Open models lower the entry cost for European startups that cannot justify training frontier systems from scratch, shifting competition toward applications, data quality and deployment.
🇨🇭 Switzerland is one of the few countries combining world-class academic compute, neutrality and a credible governance story, which makes an open Swiss model attractive to regulated buyers in health, finance and public services.
🔓 Transparency is becoming a commercial feature, not an ideological one: auditable weights and documented data lineage are exactly what compliance teams increasingly demand before signing.

For capital-efficient DeepTech founders, this is quietly significant. Research like this rarely makes headlines, but it seeds companies. 🇨🇭 A lipid switch that stops anthrax entering cells

Scientists at @EPFL have identified a lipid-based mechanism that blocks anthrax toxin, offering a new angle on how bacterial toxins hijack host cells.

Why it matters:
🧬 Host-directed approaches are attracting renewed attention because they are harder for pathogens to evade than conventional antibiotics, a market with clear unmet need and public funding behind it.
🇨🇭 Switzerland's density of structural biology, cell biology and pharma expertise means fundamental findings like this move toward translation faster here than almost anywhere else.
🛡️ Beyond anthrax, understanding the lipid machinery toxins exploit could open therapeutic routes across a family of bacterial diseases and inform biodefence programmes.

The commercial path in this field is long, but the underlying science is exactly the kind that produces durable intellectual property. Swiss biotech has repeatedly shown that small, focused teams working close to their originating lab can reach clinical relevance without the burn rates seen in larger ecosystems.

🇨🇭 Revolut bundles ChatGPT, banking meets AI distribution

Revolut is offering customers up to twelve months of free ChatGPT Go access through a tie-up with OpenAI, reports @Fintechnews Switzerland, another sign of AI moving into mainstream financial distribution.

Why it matters:
💳 Bundling AI subscriptions turns model access into a customer acquisition tool, which will compress pricing power for anyone selling generic AI assistants to consumers.
🇨🇭 Swiss financial institutions compete on trust, data residency and advisory depth rather than free add-ons, which favours specialised AI built for regulated environments over commodity chatbots.
🔁 As distribution consolidates around a few large platforms, the defensible value shifts to proprietary data, workflow integration and compliance, precisely where smaller technical teams can win.

The lesson for founders is straightforward. If your product can be replaced by a bundled subscription, it was never a company. The Swiss opportunity lies further up the stack, in systems that banks and insurers cannot buy off the shelf.
