🇨🇭 Zurich ion traps attract global quantum capital

@ZuriQ, a spin-off from @ETH Zurich, has closed a USD 25.5 million seed round led by @Quantonation to scale its ion-trap approach to quantum computing. The round is one of the largest Swiss quantum seeds to date.

Why it matters:
🔬 Seed tickets of this size signal that quantum hardware in Switzerland has moved from lab curiosity to fundable industrial roadmap, and that specialist deep tech funds are willing to underwrite long development cycles.
🇨🇭 The Swiss advantage is unusually concrete here: decades of trapped-ion and precision-engineering expertise from ETH, a dense supply chain of optics and cryogenics suppliers, and research talent that stays close to the founding institution.
⚙️ Scaling qubit counts on planar traps could shorten the path to useful error-corrected machines, and it anchors more of that value chain in Zurich rather than exporting it.

What we find notable is the capital efficiency profile: Swiss teams routinely reach hardware milestones on budgets that would barely cover a US seed extension.

🇨🇭 Rivers as infrastructure, not just scenery

Research highlighted by @EPFL News examines how rivers have shaped the growth of cities and the structure of societies over time, linking hydrology to urban form and settlement patterns.

Why it matters:
🌊 Water systems are becoming a planning and risk variable for European cities, which creates real demand for modelling, sensing and simulation tools that translate academic hydrology into decisions municipalities and insurers can act on.
🇨🇭 Switzerland sits at the top of the continent's watershed and combines that geography with world-class environmental engineering at EPFL and ETH, a rare pairing that makes the country a natural testbed for climate adaptation technology.
🏙️ The broader implication: infrastructure resilience is quietly becoming a deep tech market, spanning flood forecasting, sediment management, hydropower optimisation and urban design.

We watch these research threads closely because spin-outs in environmental modelling tend to be software-led and capital-light, exactly the profile that scales well from a Swiss base.
