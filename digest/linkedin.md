🇨🇭 A lipid switch that stops anthrax cold

Researchers at @EPFL have identified a lipid-based mechanism that blocks anthrax toxin, pointing to a new route for intervening in bacterial infection at the level of host cell biology.

Why it matters:
🧬 Host-directed approaches are one of the more interesting answers to antimicrobial resistance, because targeting the host pathway rather than the pathogen makes escape mutations far harder. That thesis is drawing serious pharma attention.
🇨🇭 Switzerland's life sciences density, with world-class academic labs sitting minutes from global pharma headquarters, shortens the distance between a mechanistic discovery and a licensing conversation. Few ecosystems compress that timeline as effectively.
💊 Biodefence and rare infection programmes also carry non-dilutive public funding and expedited regulatory pathways, which changes the capital equation for a spinout. A discovery like this can reach meaningful value inflection points on relatively modest rounds, and that is precisely where Swiss DeepTech tends to outperform.
